# Supplier strategy (standing decision, 2026-07)

## The economic rule that drives everything
Every IMPORT shipment carries ~4,000 to 5,000₪ of fixed overhead regardless of
contents: air freight (~2,500₪) + phyto cert (~400₪) + Israeli clearance
(~1,000 to 2,000₪). So: import RARELY, in BIG batches, with HIGH-VALUE contents.
Never import cheap plants to resell cheap, the overhead kills it.

## Channels by tier
### Minhui (Lynn Lin, Min Hui Fujian Horticultural) - WORKHORSE
- Reliable, comfortable, ships DHL, affordable. Variety is LIMITED.
- Use for: quantities + mid-tier requested variegated plants (e.g. Macrorrhiza
  Variegata, Thai Constellation, albos). The primary, frequent channel.

### Thailand supplier - EXCLUSIVES ONLY
- Very luxurious, very expensive: ~200 to 300₪ per plant.
- Use ONLY for ultra-exclusive varieties that can carry that price. NOT for
  regular varieties.

### Plant Hero - LAST RESORT / large luxury hauls only
- Formal air freight only, refuses DHL. High fixed cost (~4,000 to 5,000₪/shipment).
- Kat's view: their formal-only stance makes them uncompetitive for small Israeli
  orders. Use ONLY if a large consolidated luxury haul justifies the freight.
- They said their typical customer spends 70,000-80,000 RMB per order (~35,000-
  40,000₪). That is a wholesale-bulk operation, not a fit for a studio our size.
  With customs release the total is "almost double" the freight and "almost as
  much as the order" (Kat's words). Confirmed: not for us at current scale.
- Note: they confirmed the phyto cert is per-shipment (RMB 800), and the
  "Additional Declaration" inspection Kat already paid for is valid 90 days (not
  recharged). Breakdown was honest; the problem is cost/fit, not trust.

## Facebook Marketplace / cheap-plant funnel idea
- Do NOT import cheap plants for this. Source them LOCALLY in Israel (domestic
  wholesale) so there's no import overhead.
- Guard the Pink Leaf brand: it's the rare/luxury studio. If doing a cheap-plant
  hustle, treat it as a funnel (cheap plant -> get them on the list -> upsell
  rares) or keep it off the main brand name.

## Practical levers
- Use Kat's own phyto import license (cuts a middleman).
- The cheaper customs broker Kat met could shave the 1,000 to 2,000₪ clearance,
  which matters most on smaller Minhui shipments. Verify their rate.
