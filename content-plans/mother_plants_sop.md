# Mother Plant Protection SOP (stop selling the keepers)

THE PROBLEM (Kat, stated plainly): she keeps selling the best specimens, the ones
she needs as MOTHERS for corms/propagation, then has nothing left to propagate from
and has to rebuy. This bleeds money and genetics. This SOP stops it.

## THE RULE (applies BEFORE anything is listed or sold)
1. For EVERY variety, pull the single BEST specimen aside as the MOTHER:
   the prettiest, biggest, best-variegated, healthiest one.
2. Tag it physically: "MOTHER - DO NOT SELL."
3. Log it in the Notion inventory with status = MOTHER (locked, not sellable).
4. Only the REMAINING units of that variety are sellable.
5. Never sell the last one of a variety. One left = it is a mother, not stock.

## WHICH PLANTS BECOME MOTHERS FIRST (priority)
- The rare/exclusive ones that are hard or expensive to rebuy: Venom, the Cupreas,
  Ocean Mint, Jungle Mint, the Thai exclusives, anything confiscation-prone.
- The single best-variegated specimen of each variety.
- Anything Kat would be genuinely sad to lose or would have to rebuy at a loss.

## WHY (what the mothers give back)
- Propagation stock: corms, divisions, cuttings = future FREE plants.
- The grown-out showpieces for content (the big beautiful photos come from mature
  mothers, not fresh acclimating babies).
- Independence: nobody can take the collection from her.

## PRICING TIE-IN (mature vs baby)
- The FINAL price list = MATURE-plant pricing.
- Baby-size sells BELOW list (smaller + higher risk for the buyer). Rough guide:
  baby = ~50-65% of the mature list price. Never below landed cost.
- So "dropping prices" for babies is correct; do NOT drop the mature/exclusive prices.

## OPERATIONAL STEPS (do this box by box, before the next sale)
1. Go through what survived, variety by variety.
2. Pick the best of each -> tag MOTHER -> log in Notion as MOTHER.
3. Everything else of that variety -> sellable (mature = list price, baby = baby tier).
4. Keep mothers in the best growing conditions (light, humidity, warmth). They are
   the future of the business.
5. Maintain the Mother Registry in Notion: one row per variety with the mother flagged.
