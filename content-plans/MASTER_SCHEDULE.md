## Best imagery for Pink Leaf (the answer to "what should I post")
Rank of what actually performs for a rare-plant brand, use these:
1. ROTATING / SPINNING plant videos (360 of a stunning plant) - the #1 scroll-stopper. Lead with these.
2. Macro close-ups of variegation, new growth, roots - hypnotic, shows quality.
3. New-leaf unfurl timelapse - the "reveal" dopamine hit.
4. Grow-room / "the lab" shots - builds authority (no face needed).
5. Hands working: potting, corms, trimming - satisfying process content.
6. Before/after growth - proof you grow them well.
7. Unboxing imports - excitement + behind the scenes.
Outdoor plant photos: ONLY for the "growing outside" educational post. Not the core look.
Keep it all in the cream + pink aesthetic, natural light or your pink grow light. Real, never stock.
