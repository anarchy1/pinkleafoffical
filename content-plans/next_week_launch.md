## THE ACCLIMATION VIDEO (faceless, ~60-90s reel)
Needs: distilled/RO water (good call - no chlorine/minerals to stress fragile TC roots),
Fluval Stratum, clean cups, sterile tools, the TC baby, a humidity cover.
1. Setup laid out. Text: "acclimating a tissue culture baby 🌱"
2. Rinse the old gel off the roots in distilled water. Text: "first, rinse off the old gel"
3. Trim any rot at the base, sterile tools. Text: "clean cut, only healthy tissue stays"
4. Fill cup with Fluval pre-moistened with distilled water. Text: "into pre-moistened Fluval"
5. Nestle the plant: roots in, base supported, leaves off the walls. Text: "settled, not buried"
6. Cover for humidity, bright indirect light. Text: "high humidity, soft light"
7. Text: "now don't fertilize, don't disturb. just wait 🩷"
Caption: nothing like watching them grow. this is how we give them the best start.

## Buy list for the video / acclimation
- Distilled or RO water
- Confirm you have enough Fluval (rough: 12-15 bags of 8L for ~300 babies pure, or 6-8 bags if mixed 50/50 with perlite/pumice)
- Perlite / pumice / LECA for grow-on mixes
