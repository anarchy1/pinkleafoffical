positioning as a "lab" + showing his face. Kat can out-position him because she's actually

## The cloning / lab plan (the real long-term moat)
Goal: partner with a tissue-culture lab to mass-propagate Kat's best mother plants, so
Pink Leaf becomes THE source in Israel, not an importer. Can't do TC from an apartment
(needs a sterile lab), so contract it out. Kat does corm/pup division herself in the meantime (free).

### Two propagation paths (important technical reality)
1. Corms / pups / division - Kat does this herself, now, free. Best for the chimeral pink
   variegated plants, because highly variegated (chimeral) plants often do NOT come true
   from tissue culture (the variegation can be lost/reverted). Keep these as mothers, harvest corms.
2. Lab tissue culture (contract) - best for stable-variegation and non-chimeral rares, for scale.

### Sunday: questions to ask the TC labs
1. Do you do contract/custom TC propagation from client-supplied mother plants?
2. What material do you need (healthy tissue, meristem), and do you virus-index it?
3. Minimum batch size, and full timeline (initiation to multiplication to rooting to ready)?
4. Cost per plantlet / per batch.
5. EXCLUSIVITY - will you propagate my varieties for anyone else? (Critical. Without a
   contract they can clone your plants for your competitors.)
6. Your success rate specifically with variegated Alocasia / aroids?

### Economics logic
Import cost per plant is high (supplier + shipping + customs + losses). TC unit cost at scale
is very low once established. The moat is owning the variety + exclusivity + being the local source.

## Preorders (stop being shy about this - it's the safest money there is)
People want to reserve plants on paper. Say yes, with structure:
- Preorder = deposit up front (e.g., 30-50%), balance before it ships.
- Deposit is what turns "promise" into real money and de-risks the reorder.
- It also tells you demand BEFORE you buy stock, so you stop guessing.
- Simple system: a preorder list (name, plant, deposit paid, balance due, ETA).
