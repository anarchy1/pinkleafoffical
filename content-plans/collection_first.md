# Collection First (the #1 rule) - durable

The problem this solves: Kat keeps selling plants that were supposed to be her own
keepers/mothers, then has to rebuy them. This stops that.

## THE RULE
Every time a shipment arrives, BEFORE anything goes up for sale:
1. Pull ONE of each variety into the "Keeper" pile, marked DO NOT SELL.
2. That keeper = Kat's collection + the future mother plant for propagation.
3. Only the remaining units are sellable.
4. Never sell the last one of a variety. If only one exists, it's a keeper, not stock.

## Why this is priority #1
- The collection is the foundation: it's future mother stock (free propagation), the
  showpiece plants for content (grown-out keepers = the big beautiful photos), and the
  thing nobody can take from her.
- Selling keepers for short-term cash and rebuying later loses money and genetics.

## Where the actual keeper list lives
Notion (inventory), not the repo (no stock quantities in the repo). Claude maintains the
keeper list in Notion: one flagged keeper per variety, updated each shipment.
