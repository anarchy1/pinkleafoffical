# Quote / empowerment post series (content idea)

Idea (Kat, 2026): a recurring series of inspirational + lightly-pointed quote posts
for Instagram. Cheap to make, shareable, and channels the "someone tried to copy/
steal the business" frustration into brand confidence WITHOUT naming anyone.

## Rules
- Public = classy and unnamed. Plant metaphors make them clever, not bitter.
- Never name Luma or any person. The confident original never punches down.
- On-brand look: cream / dark-green background, serif type, small Pink Leaf mark.
- Post as square or story/reel; rotate one every few days between plant + care content.

## Originals-vs-copies (the pointed ones, no names)
- They can copy the leaves. They can't copy the roots.
- Originals get copied. That's how you know you're the original.
- You can buy the same plant. You can't buy the years it took to grow it.
- Copy the catalog all you want, you still can't copy the love it's grown with.
- Grow your own, or admit you're just repotting someone else's.
- The rarest things can't be duplicated. Neither can I.

## Warmer / inspirational
- Slow growth is still growth.
- Every rare leaf was once just a hopeful cutting.
- Bloom on your own time, not on anyone else's.
- Roots grow in the dark long before anyone sees the leaf.
