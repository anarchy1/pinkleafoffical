## Video length rules (Instagram vs Facebook)

- **Instagram Reels:** up to 3 minutes. Best reach is 15 to 60 seconds.
  Instagram has no long-form anymore, so a tutorial longer than 3 minutes gets
  split into Part 1 / Part 2. Stories are 60 seconds per segment.
- **Facebook:** feed video can be hours long. Facebook Reels max about 90 seconds.
  So Facebook can hold the full uncut tutorial in one piece.
- **Standard move:** film once. Cut a tight 60 to 90 second Reel for Instagram
  (that is where the reach is), post the full uncut version on Facebook feed.
  Only split into parts if the Instagram cut has to run long.

## POST 1 - "Leave your plant alone" (do and don't)

Purpose: stop losses from customers who ignore care instructions, and stop Kat
having to feel bad or compensate when a customer causes the death themselves.
Format: carousel of do/don't pairs. Tone: firm but kind, educational.

Do / Don't pairs:
- DON'T repot the second it arrives. DO leave it in the substrate it came in for at least a month.
- DON'T swap the substrate because it "looks like it is struggling." DO wait, travel stress looks scary and passes.
- DON'T add fertilizer at the start. DO trust the substrate, it already feeds the plant.
- DON'T think the substrate is just carbon or filler. DO know it is a mineral lava substrate, a living feed. That is the whole point.
- DON'T panic and "rescue" it. DO message us first, we will tell you if anything is actually wrong.

Closing line idea: "Almost every plant that arrives is fine. The biggest risk to
a new rare plant is being fussed with. Give it a month, trust the process, and it
will reward you."

Key facts to weave in (from real customer confusion):
- The Botanique substrate is a volcanic lava mineral substrate. It is NOT carbon.
- It already feeds the plant, so you do not add fertilizer at first. The substrate IS the feed.
- Swapping substrate on a stressed young / TC plant on arrival is the number one
  avoidable cause of death. The "rescue" instinct kills more than it saves.

## POST 2 - Planting tissue culture into substrate (step by step)

Purpose: educational reel/carousel. Kat narrates. Plan the running order so it is
not a mess on camera.

Running order:
1. Unpack and inspect the TC baby, gently rinse the old gel off the roots.
2. Sterile tools, trim any rot as close to the base as possible.
3. Prep the lava mineral substrate.
4. Plant it in, roots settled, base supported, leaves not touching the sides.
5. Into the humidity cup, bright indirect light.
6. The wait: no fertilizer, no disturbing, watch for new growth. Good drainage, no soggy, no rot.

Status: running order set. Next step is a one-line talking script per step so Kat
can read them off while filming.
