# Weekly series: "הצמחים שלנו אצלכם" (Pink Leaf at Home)

A recurring, once-a-week post featuring customers' plants thriving in their homes.
Post however many photos came in that week (flexible count).

## Format
- Carousel of the customer photos received that week (1 to many).
- Same caption template every week (below), so it reads as a consistent series.
- Same series hashtag every week so all editions are findable: #פינקליף_אצלכם

## Rules
- Get a quick okay from each customer before showing their home/plant.
- Keep it its OWN post. Do NOT merge with a single client's review/testimonial
  (mixing one client's words with other clients' homes isn't fair to either).
- Anonymize if the customer prefers (crop out any identifying info cleanly, no messy blur).

## Reusable caption template (fill in / post as-is)
הצמחים שלנו אצלכם 🌿🩷

כל שבוע אנחנו מארחים כאן את הצמחים שלכם, אצל מי שבאמת אוהב אותם.
תודה לכל מי ששלח, אתם עושים לנו את השבוע 💚

יש לכם צמח פינק ליף שגדל יפה? שלחו לנו תמונה ואולי הוא יככב בפוסט הבא 🌸

Our plants, in your homes 🌿🩷 Every week we feature your plants, with the people who
truly love them. Thank you to everyone who sent photos. Got a Pink Leaf plant growing
beautifully? Send us a photo and it might star in next week's post 🌸

#פינקליף_אצלכם #pinkleaf #rareplants #צמחיםנדירים #plantsofisrael

## Note
The closing line ("send us your photo") grows the pipeline, more submissions = more to
post next week. The series volume depends on how many customers send in.
