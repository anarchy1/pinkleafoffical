# Business reset + structure plan (2026-08)

Context: the Thai (Jakkrit) live-plant shipment had a very low survival rate. Whole
half-boxes lost. The plants that DIED were the expensive bare-root established ones.
The CHEAP TC + corms survived ~100% (same acclimation, same hands, gray box ~100%).
Conclusion: the loss is the shipping method + plant format, NOT Kat's care.

## THE COMPASS (new sourcing rule)
- Import what SURVIVES: TC plants and corms (young, hardy, ship well, ~100% survival).
- Stop importing what DIES: expensive bare-root established plants over long transit.
- If a fragile/expensive variety is worth it, change the shipping (faster courier / air,
  smaller size) BEFORE ordering, or don't order it.
- Kat's acclimation is proven excellent. Give it good arrivals and it works.

## PRIORITY ORDER (work top to bottom)
1. TRIAGE THE SURVIVORS (now)
   - Judge by crown + roots, not leaves. Firm crown + white roots (or a new spear) = keep.
     Mushy center / black roots = write off. In-between: give a few days, new growth = alive.
   - Make a clean list of what actually survived and is sellable/giftable.
2. LOSS CALCULATION (revenue lost to the die-off)
   - For each dead plant: its SELL price (not cost) = lost revenue; its landed COST = cash lost.
   - Needs from Kat: which plants died, and how many. Claude has the Thai landed costs.
3. THAI ORDER DECISION
   - Do NOT complete/reorder the fragile expensive bare-root plants that die in transit.
   - Complete only with the varieties that arrived fine this shipment (the hardy ones).
   - No point paying for plants that die on the way.
4. ORGANIZE INVENTORY (single source of truth)
   - One live Notion inventory. Every survivor logged. Every sale registers against it.
   - Save every order/receipt to Notion the moment it arrives (stop losing them).
5. CARD PAYMENTS (Invoice4u)
   - Kat: finish Invoice4u signup, generate the hosted payment link/button in the account.
   - Claude: wire that link into the product Buy buttons on the site (replaces WhatsApp routing).
   - Go live only after inventory is verified clean.
