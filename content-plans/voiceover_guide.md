# How to do voiceover for your own videos (no face needed)

## The workflow (do it in this order)
1. WRITE the script first (in Notes). Short lines, one idea each, start with a hook.
2. FILM the video silently (hands, plants, process).
3. RECORD the voiceover over the footage.
4. ADD captions too (most people watch on mute).

## Where to record the voiceover (pick one)
- Instagram Reels / TikTok: both have a built-in Voiceover tool (mic icon). Import the clip,
  tap record, narrate while it plays. Zero extra apps.
- CapCut (free, what most creators use): import clip -> Audio -> Voiceover -> record. Easiest
  to edit, redo lines, and add captions. Recommended.

## How to sound good (phone mic is enough)
- Quiet room with soft stuff (curtains, bed, even a closet) kills echo.
- Hold the phone about a hand's width away, slightly to the side (avoids popping).
- Warm, calm, like talking to ONE friend. Don't "perform."
- Smile while you talk - it warms the voice.
- Go a touch slower than feels natural.
- Record line by line, redo any you flub, stitch them.
- Know the script so it doesn't sound read.

## Language
Hebrew, natural and casual, your real voice. Authenticity beats polish.

## If you hate your recorded voice
Everyone does at first, it passes. Your real voice builds more trust than text ever will.
Fallback: text-only captions. But try the voice - it's your authority, no camera required.
