# Drop visual references here

This is the folder Kat asked for. Anything that shows how Pink Leaf looks, or
where it is going, goes in this directory.

## What belongs here

- Logo versions, current and past
- Sticker designs, printed and unprinted
- WhatsApp sticker packs
- Packaging, labels, cards, anything printed
- Screenshots of layouts or posts worth keeping as a reference
- Rough ideas and sketches. Unfinished is fine, that is the point

## How to get files in

**From the Mac:** drop the files in this folder, then commit and push. No
naming rules, a descriptive filename is enough.

**From the phone:** send them in the chat. They get filed here with a name that
says what they are. This is usually faster than the WhatsApp round trip.

## Naming, loosely

`what-it-is-variant.ext`, lowercase, hyphens. For example
`sticker-thankyou-whatsapp.png` or `logo-round-v2.jpg`. Close enough is fine.
Being able to find it later matters more than being tidy.

## Rule

A reference is not a brand asset. Finished, approved assets live one level up
in `brand/` and are listed in `brand/README.md`. This folder is the inbox.
Nothing here should be used on the site or on a card until it has been promoted.
