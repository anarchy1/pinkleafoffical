### English

```
Philodendron Pink Princess Marble 🌸

The standard Pink Princess puts its pink down in one large block. The Marble
does it differently: pink, silver and green mix inside the same leaf, and no
two leaves come out alike.

Now the thing worth knowing before you buy one.

The pink has no chlorophyll. It makes the plant no energy at all. A leaf that
is entirely pink looks perfect in a photograph, and in reality it cannot feed
itself, and eventually it dries.

The healthy plant is the one holding green and pink in the same leaf. That is
why the marbled pattern is worth more than a solid pink leaf, not less.

And what decides how much pink you keep? Light. Too little and the plant
reverts to green to survive. Direct sun burns the pink off in days. Bright
indirect light, and steady.
```

### Why this angle

The obvious caption for this plant is how pretty it is, which the photograph
already says better than words can. The variegation trade-off is the thing a
customer does not know, it justifies the price without mentioning a price,
and it means the first green leaf reads as the plant being healthy rather
than as a disappointment.
