### English
```
The plant does not break in transit. It breaks the week after, from what worry makes you do to it. 📦

A plant in a box has been in the dark, at the wrong temperature, unable to photosynthesize, for one to five days. It arrives running on reserves. Everything in the first 48 hours is about letting it refill them.

Open it the day it arrives, even at night. Lift it by the pot, never by the stem.

Then do nothing dramatic. Do not repot. Do not fertilize. Do not move it into the sun to "give it energy". Each of those is a second stress, and stacked stresses are what actually kills a shipped plant.

Check two things only: the substrate (dry means water it, wet means leave it) and the base of the stem (firm means the plant is fine, whatever the leaves look like).

Completely normal: the bottom leaf yellowing and dropping, a drooping petiole that lifts within two days, a crisp edge on an old leaf. A plant shedding its oldest leaf while pushing a new one is a healthy plant.

Not normal: a soft base, a sour smell, a leaf that goes translucent and collapses within hours. Send us a photo the same day.

And quarantine for 14 days away from your other plants. Yes, including plants you bought from us.
```
