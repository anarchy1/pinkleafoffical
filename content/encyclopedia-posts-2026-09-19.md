### English
```
Most rare plants that die in your home did not starve. They were overfed. 🧪

A plant in bark and perlite has no pantry. Everything it eats comes from you, which is exactly why the dose is the whole game.

The rule: a quarter of the label dose, at every watering, all season.
The numbers: EC 0.6 to 0.9 for a mature plant. 0.3 to 0.5 for a TC start or a freshly hardened plant. Never above 1.2.
Israeli tap water already carries calcium and magnesium. On RO or distilled, you must add Cal-Mag.

And every 4 to 6 weeks: flush with clean water. That white crust on the substrate is not mold, it is salt.

Full guide, including our dosing protocol 👇
```

### English
```
You don't have a pest problem. You have a quarantine problem. 🔍

Every infestation we have ever treated came in on a plant that skipped quarantine. Not from the window. Not from "the air". From a new plant, put next to the collection on day one.

So the protocol starts before the pest: 14 days alone for every new arrival, in a different room if you can. No exceptions, including plants from us.

Four pests matter in an Israeli home:
Thrips, silver streaks and scars that are permanent on white tissue
Spider mites, which explode in August in front of an air conditioner
Mealybugs, white cotton in the leaf axils
Fungus gnats, which are not a pest but a symptom. Gnats mean you are overwatering.
```

### Hebrew

```
בכל החלפת עציץ אתם כנראה זורקים צמחים שלמים לפח. 🥔

כל אלוקסיה בריאה בונה בשקט פקעות לאורך קנה השורש: בצלים קטנים בקליפה חומה, בגודל של גרגר חומוס עד אגוז לוז. כל אחת מהן היא צמח שלם שמחכה לאות.
```

### English
```
At every repot you are probably throwing whole plants in the bin. 🥔

Every healthy Alocasia quietly builds corms along the rhizome: small brown-husked bulbs, chickpea to hazelnut sized. Each one is a complete plant waiting for a signal.

How to wake them:
Peel the brown husk from base to tip, never nicking the growing point
Clear box, damp (not wet) sphagnum, corms tip-up and half buried
25 to 28°C, humidity near 90%, lid open for a minute every two days
Roots in 1 to 3 weeks. First leaf in 3 to 6 weeks

And the uncomfortable part: a corm from a variegated Alocasia is not a guaranteed variegated plant. Variegation is a chimera in the growing point, and each corm carries its own copy of it, or does not. Anyone selling you "variegated" corms is selling you a bet.

Full protocol 👇
```
