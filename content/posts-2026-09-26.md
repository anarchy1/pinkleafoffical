### English

```
Philodendron Tortum 🦴

In Chinese it is called "fish bone", and nothing has ever described it better.

This is not a leaf with holes in it. This is a leaf cut so deeply that only the
skeleton is left. Every new leaf twists differently from the one before it, and
no two repeat.

It comes from northern Brazil, around Manaus, and from Bolivia. In the wild it
climbs tree trunks in the understory, which is why it wants a support pole,
bright indirect light and an airy mix.

Very sensitive to overwatering. Water only once the top layer has dried.

Swipe for the full story 👉

Message us for price and availability 💬
```
