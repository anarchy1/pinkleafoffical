# True Health - Blooming Tea (marketing foundation)

Separate venture from Pink Leaf (plants). Stored here for durability until it gets
its own home. Product: blooming / flowering tea (a hand-tied flower that unfurls in
the cup), dropshipped. Model: ~$20 landed cost, sell ~$60, ~$40 gross margin.
Reference that works: @FlorAmora (15K+ likes, 2.5K saves on the bloom-reveal video).

## Why this works and why Kat specifically wins
- The product sells itself through ONE mesmerizing video: the flower blooming in glass.
- That is organic-content-driven, which is Kat's actual superpower (she already did it with plants).
- Organic content also PROTECTS the $40 margin. Paid ads would eat most of it; organic keeps it.
- So: organic-video-first. Ads only later, once a video proves it converts.

## Margin reality
- $60 sell - $20 landed = $40 gross per order. Good.
- Protect it: sell organic (no ad spend), disclose shipping time honestly, nail the unboxing.
- Watch-outs: long China shipping = complaints. Mitigate with clear delivery expectations,
  and later hold small local stock of the hero bundle.

## Positioning
Umbrella brand: True Health (wellness rituals). Hero product: blooming tea.
Sell it on three feelings, in this priority:
1. Beauty / wow (the bloom reveal) - this is the hook that stops the scroll.
2. Gift (give her flowers she can drink) - this is the reason to buy.
3. Wellness ritual (calming, light, aids digestion) - this is the reason to reorder.

## Product page copy (paste-ready)
Headline: A garden that blooms in your cup.
Sub: Hand-tied blooming tea that unfurls into a living flower as it steeps. Light, floral, calming.
Body: Drop it in hot water and watch a flower open in front of you. Each one is hand-tied from
real tea leaves and dried blossoms. Gentle, low-caffeine, soothing for digestion, and the most
beautiful gift you can actually drink.
Bundle: Blooming tea set + borosilicate glass teapot, so the bloom is the whole show.
Bullets: hand-tied real flowers - low caffeine - soothing ritual - stunning gift - reusable glass pot

## Viral hooks (organic video, film the bloom)
1. The reveal: pour hot water, timelapse the flower opening. Text: "the flower hidden in your tea."
2. Gift: "give her flowers she can actually drink." (couples / gifting audience)
3. Ritual: "my 3-minute reset every night." (wellness audience)
4. ASMR: slow pour + bloom, no words, just sound and light.
5. POV: "POV: your tea blooms into a flower."
