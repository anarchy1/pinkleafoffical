# True Health - blooming tea, first 5 organic video scripts

All filmable with a phone. The bloom is the star. No face needed. Shoot the pour in
bright natural light against a clean background. Vertical.

## 1. The reveal (the money shot - model on FlorAmora)
Shot: glass teapot, drop the tea ball in, pour hot water, hold on the flower opening (speed it up).
Text on screen: "the flower hidden in your tea, revealed."
Caption: A whole flower blooms in your cup 🌸 hand-tied blooming tea. Link in bio.

## 2. Gift angle (highest intent to buy)
Shot: hands placing the teapot in front of someone, the bloom opening.
Text: "give her flowers she can actually drink."
Caption: The gift that blooms in front of them 🩷 blooming tea + glass pot set.

## 3. Ritual / wellness (drives reorders)
Shot: dim, cozy, evening light, slow pour, steam.
Text: "my 3-minute reset. every night."
Caption: Low caffeine, calming, gentle on digestion. Your new wind-down 🌙

## 4. ASMR (pure reach, no words)
Shot: extreme close-up, slow pour, sound of water, the flower unfurling. Silence + natural sound.
Text: none, or a tiny "🌸".
Caption: sound on 🔊

## 5. POV / trend format
Shot: first-person, place the ball, pour, watch it bloom.
Text: "POV: your tea turns into a flower."
Caption: still not over this 🌸 blooming tea, link in bio.

## Notes
- Post 1 (the reveal) first. It's the proven format.
- Repost the best performer to Reels + Facebook + TikTok.
- No paid ads until one organic video clearly converts. Protect the margin.
