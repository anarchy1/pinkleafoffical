## Terms (from the catalog)
- Rare TC group import. Minimum ₪30 per plant. Prices are dynamic; a closing
  date is set per round. Customs and phytosanitary delays are possible.
- Price includes everything: plant cost, shipping, customs, logistics, and
  risk coverage.
- Acclimation service (optional): +₪100 per plant for 4 weeks of professional
  acclimation in the studio, and then acclimation success is on us. Otherwise
  the plant ships as an unacclimated "plug" and acclimation risk is on the buyer.
- Payment in full, upfront. Full refund if the order does not arrive.
- Prices computed at a fixed rate of ₪3.00 per USD.
- Estimated delivery: 21 to 35 days from when the order is placed with the supplier.
- No specific variegation pattern is guaranteed per plug.
- No cancellations after the order is placed with the supplier. All sales final.
- Contact: pinkleaf.co.il, contact@pinkleaf.co.il, 055-911-6990. Bit | PayBox.
